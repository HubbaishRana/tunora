Privacy Policy

Tunora - Offline Music Player · Last updated: 19 November 2026

Summary

Tunora does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no account and no ads. Everything stays on your phone.

What the app accesses on your device

  • Music and audio files (permission READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, or storage access on Android 12 and older): used only to find and play the songs on your device and to show their title, artist, album and cover art. Files are never uploaded or changed.
  • Audio settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): used only for the equalizer, bass booster, virtualizer, volume booster and reverb.
  • Background playback (foreground service and wake lock): keeps your music playing with a notification while the screen is off.

Data stored on your device

Your playlists, favourites, play counts, recent searches, equalizer settings and last playback position are saved locally inside the app so it works the way you left it. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Data collection and sharing

Tunora collects no personal or usage data and shares no data with third parties. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app.

Ratings

Tunora may show Google Play's built-in rating prompt. That prompt is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; Tunora receives no information from it.

Children

Tunora is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if ads are added in a future version), the new version will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date will change before the update is released.
